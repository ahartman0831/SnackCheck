# Catalog evidence pilot 001

Reviewed: 2026-08-31

Purpose: turn ten USDA shortlist leads into a conservative human-review packet. This
research does not approve, publish, or promote a product. A signed-in SnackCheck reviewer
must still compare the proposed text with the linked manufacturer evidence and confirm the
promotion one product at a time.
